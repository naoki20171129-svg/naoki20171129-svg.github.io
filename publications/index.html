  1. bioRxiv
    Effects of microgravity on the three-dimensional morphology of rhizoids in Physcomitrium patens
    Naoki Yagihara, Takahisa Wakabayashi, Ryohei Yamaura, and 15 more authors
    bioRxiv, 2026
    Preprint
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    Rooting systems of plants perceive environmental stimuli and flexibly regulate their growth. Analyzing rhizoid morphology under microgravity, where gravitational effects are largely eliminated, provides an effective approach to examine the gravity-response mechanisms that evolved after terrestrialization. To elucidate microgravity effects on rhizoid growth of Physcomitrium patens, we analyzed 3D datasets obtained by refraction-contrast micro-CT using synchrotron radiation after fixation and embedding of samples from the Space Moss experiment conducted on the International Space Station. We optimized a WEKA-based machine-learning segmentation approach, and morphological analyses revealed that, compared with both ground and artificial 1 × g conditions, rhizoid elongation and gravitropic responses were suppressed under microgravity, leading to reduced vertical growth.
